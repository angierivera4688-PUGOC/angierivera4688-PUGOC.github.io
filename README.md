# Household Budget

A simple, private household budget app. Track your fixed costs, savings goals
and daily spending, and see how much you lose on "dead expenses" (impulse and
small recurring spending).

## Features
- Daily expense log (needed vs. dead expenses)
- Fixed costs, subscriptions and debts
- Savings goals and the 52-week savings challenge
- Monthly overview with budget by category
- 4 color styles
- Works offline

## Privacy
All data is stored only on your device. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.
See the [privacy policy](privacidad.html).

# Presupuesto del Hogar

App sencilla y privada para llevar el presupuesto de tu casa: gastos fijos,
ahorros y gasto diario, con control de gastos muertos. Tus datos se guardan
solo en tu dispositivo.
